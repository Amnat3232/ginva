## 📊 Parameter Matrix (Hybrid Approach)

| Parameter                 | Value             | Rationale                   |
| ------------------------- | ----------------- | --------------------------- |
| **LTV Tiers**             | 20%, 40%, 60%     | Proven safe ratios          |
| **Durations**             | 30, 90, 380 days  | Fixed for predictability    |
| **Interest Rates**        | 2.5%, 2.25%, 2.0% | Decreases with duration     |
| **Protocol Fee**          | 0.5%              | Competitive but sustainable |
| **Liquidation Threshold** | 20% Health Factor | Conservative but fair       |
| **Keeper Reward**         | 5%                | Sufficient incentive        |
| **Min Loan Size**         | $1,000 USDC       | Prevent spam                |
| **Max Loan Size**         | $10,000 USDC      | Manage risk                 |

## 🔍 Risk Mitigation Strategy

- ✅ **Conservative LTV caps** prevent over-leverage
- ✅ **Fixed interest rates** prevent economic gaming
- ✅ **Health monitoring** catch risky positions early
- ✅ **Insurance fund** protect against systemic risks

### Technical Risks

- ✅ **Simple codebase** reduces bug surface area
- ✅ **Proven patterns** leverage battle-tested approaches
- ✅ **Comprehensive testing** before mainnet launch
- ✅ **Gradual upgrades** limit blast radius

### Market Risks

- ✅ **Competitive rates** attract initial users
- ✅ **Enhanced features** create long-term moat
- ✅ **Community governance** ensure protocol alignment
- ✅ **Performance tracking** enable rapid iteration
