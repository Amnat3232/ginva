# I Built a DeFi Protocol After My Biggest Regret

*How a father's impossible choice became a lending protocol that gives people options*

## The Moment Everything Changed

Early 2025. Crypto market crashing. My child's tuition was due.

I had ETH. ETH I believed in. ETH I knew would come back.

But my daughter needed money *now*.

So I sold. At a loss. At the bottom.

It was the most painful financial decision I've ever made.

**If Ginva existed back then, I would have deposited that ETH, borrowed USDC to pay the tuition, and gotten my ETH back a month later when things recovered.**

Instead, I lost thousands in unrealized gains. Forever.

That's why I built Ginva.

## What is Ginva?

Ginva is a decentralized lending protocol on Solana that lets you borrow USDC against your crypto — **without selling it**.

Think of it like a modern, transparent blockchain-based pawnshop:

```
You: "I need cash but don't want to sell my crypto"
Traditional DeFi: ❌ Liquidated at 3am with no warning
GINVA: ✅ 72-hour grace period before liquidation
```

### The Problem Ginva Solves

| Situation | Other DeFi | Ginva |
|-----------|-----------|-------|
| Price drops 5% | Instant liquidation 💣 | 72h grace period 🛡️ |
| Need cash urgently | Forced to sell at loss | Borrow against collateral |
| Hidden fees | 3-25% variable 👀 | 8% fixed forever 👀 |
| AI Agents | Manual keepers only | AI agents can earn SOL |

---

## Why 72 Hours Matters

Here's what happens in DeFi today:

1. Your collateral is worth $1,000
2. You borrow $700 (70% LTV)
3. Price drops 8%
4. **Liquitdated immediately.** You lose everything.

**In Ginva:**

1. Your collateral is worth $1,000
2. You borrow $700 (70% LTV)
3. Price drops 8%
4. **You get 72 HOURS to react:**
   - Add more collateral
   - Repay part of the loan
   - Or let it liquidate (still 72h later)

That 72 hours? That's the difference between losing everything and keeping your assets.

## The Technical Stuff

For the builders out there:

- **Network**: Solana (devnet live, mainnet coming)
- **Language**: Rust (Pinocchio framework)
- **Oracle**: Pyth for price feeds
- **Security**: Reentrancy guards, circuit breakers, emergency pause
- **Fees**: 0% deposit/withdraw, 0.30% borrow, 0.50% liquidation
