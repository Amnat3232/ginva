## Known Risks
1. Liquidation Risk - Positions can be liquidated if undercollateralized
2. Oracle Risk - Price feeds can be manipulated 
3. Smart Contract Risk - Bugs could lead to loss of funds
4. No Insurance - Protocol has no insurance fund
